Method and literature

Interpret the estimates in the context of the source protocol and model limits.

Model: standardized Ridge regression using formulation descriptors and specimen radius. Same-curve force, torque, gap, and stress summaries are excluded from model inputs to prevent target leakage.

Experimental reference: Heidenreich et al. report hydrated rheometer testing at 37 °C with three compression–tension cycles to 15% strain at 40 μm/s, and identify modified one-term Ogden mechanics parameters by inverse finite-element fitting.

Data source: Faber et al., experimental hydrogel curves for OHA-GEL, ADA-GEL, and alginate, Zenodo record 10.5281/zenodo.18171138 (CC BY 4.0).
